# Q*thulhu

**Lovecraft Arcade Vol. 5** — A Dosswerks Arcade Game

A Q*bert-inspired arcade game set in the impossible geometry of R'lyeh. Play as Q*thulhu, a tiny Star-Spawn hatchling, hopping across cubes while dodging Cthulhu's tentacle eruptions and eldritch enemies.

## How to Play

- **Arrow Keys** (or WASD): Hop diagonally across cubes
- **Swipe** (mobile): Swipe in diagonal directions to hop
- Convert all cubes to the target color to clear each level
- Avoid tentacle eruptions (watch for green pulsing warnings)
- Catch the Elder Sign to freeze and destroy enemies and tentacles
- Jump onto Night Gaunts at board edges to escape back to the top
- Destroy Cultists by hopping into them
- Extra life awarded every 10,000 points

## Game Elements

### Enemies

- **Deep Ones**: Basic enemies with predictable downward movement (Level 1+)
- **Mi-Go**: Fly in from odd directions, hop erratically, then fly away (Level 2+)
- **Cultists**: Revert converted cubes back to neutral — destroyable by Q*thulhu (Level 2+)
- **Baby Shoggoths**: Crawl along the side faces of cubes on the pyramid edges (Level 3+)
- **Star Spawn**: Aggressive enemies that pursue the player (Level 4+)

### Hazards

- **Tentacle Eruptions**: Cthulhu's tentacles erupt from cubes, telegraphed by green pulsing
- **The Alert**: A ship that crosses the water below the pyramid, firing cannonballs at random cubes (fires from Level 3+)
- **Ambient Tentacles**: Decorative tentacles flanking the pyramid, implying Cthulhu's presence

### Allies & Power-ups

- **Night Gaunts**: Escape transports hovering at board edges — jump to them to ride back to the top
- **Elder Sign**: Freezes all enemies and tentacles, allowing Q*thulhu to destroy them by hopping on them (one per level, appears after enemies are on the board)

### Difficulty Progression

- **Levels 1–4**: Single-hop cube conversion
- **Level 5+**: Two-stage cube conversion (neutral → intermediate → converted)
- Enemy spawn rates and variety increase each level
- Ship fires more frequently at higher levels
